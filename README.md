# 🚪 Roblox Doors Desktop Entities (Standalone Executables)

A collection of interactive Windows desktop entities inspired by the popular Roblox horror game **DOORS**. These are compiled standalone executable files (`.exe`), so **no Python installation or setup is required** to run them.

---

## 🎮 Included Entities

### 1. 🟢 Rush (`Rush.exe`)
Rush lurks in your desktop environment, warning you with flickering screen light brightness and sound cues before rushing across your screen.

* **Flicker Warning:** Screen brightness flickers dynamically when Rush approaches.
* **Desktop Shortcuts:** Automatically creates `Closet.lnk` on your **Desktop** and `Cam_Crucifix_Bao_Ve.lnk` in your **Downloads** folder.
* **Survival Mechanics:**
  * **Hide in Closet:** Double-click `Closet.lnk` on your Desktop to hide inside a closet while Rush zooms past your screen through the door crack.
  * **Use Crucifix:** Double-click `Cam_Crucifix_Bao_Ve.lnk` from Downloads to hold the Crucifix, trapping and banishing Rush into the floor with magic seals and glowing chains.
* **Failure Penalty:** Failing to hide or use the Crucifix results in a static ghost phase followed by a jumpscare.

### 2. 🔴 A-90 (`A-90.exe`)
Inspired by the Rooms / Hotel mechanics, A-90 tests your reaction time and discipline.

* **Sudden Appearance:** Appears randomly on screen with its signature warning sound and visual stop-sign prompt.
* **Strict Rule:** **DO NOT MOVE YOUR MOUSE** or interact with any window while A-90 is active.
* **Outcome:** Staying completely still makes A-90 fade away safely. Any mouse movement triggers an immediate damage/jumpscare event.

## 📥 Installation & Running

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page (or download the project folder).
2. Extract the `.zip` archive to any location on your PC.
3. Open the folder and double-click the desired `.exe` file (`Rush.exe` or `A-90.exe`).

> ⚠️ **Important Note:** Do not move the `.exe` file out of its extracted folder! It requires the adjacent `_internal` and `assets` folders to load texture files and audio clips properly.

## 🛠️ System Requirements

* **OS:** Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)

## 📜 Controls & Hotkeys

* **`ESC` Key:** Instantly close/terminate the running entity program.
* **`Closet.lnk` (Desktop):** Triggers closet hiding sequence (Rush).
* **`Cam_Crucifix_Bao_Ve.lnk` (Downloads):** Equips Crucifix (Rush).
